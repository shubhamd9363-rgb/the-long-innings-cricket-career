# Game Design — The Long Innings

## Product promise
Build a cricketer's career from an unknown rookie to a meaningful personal legacy. This is a career/life simulation—not a match-only arcade game.

## Core loop
1. Review the career dashboard and upcoming opportunity.
2. Train, recover, manage resources, or prepare tactically.
3. Play a match/trial with decisions that affect outcomes.
4. Receive a scorecard and performance assessment.
5. Update skills, fitness, finances, reputation, and selection prospects.
6. Advance the calendar; encounter new opportunities and setbacks.

## Design pillars
- **Earned progression:** selection depends on relevant performance, role, eligibility, fitness, competition, and team needs.
- **Agency:** player choices affect match and career outcomes.
- **Readable consequences:** feedback explains why an outcome happened and what can improve.
- **Viable careers:** domestic, franchise, specialist, and international paths can all be satisfying.
- **Authentic structure, fictional world:** fictional people/teams by default; competition names and eligibility are configurable.

## First playable vertical slice (Milestone 1)
Player creation → career home → training → complete interactive T20 → scorecard/stat updates → assessment → local save/restore.

## Initial assumptions
- Single-player, fictional generated ecosystem.
- Seedable match simulation for reproducible tests.
- Browser-first; offline/PWA and Android installability are release goals.
- No real-money purchases, gambling, or pay-to-win.
